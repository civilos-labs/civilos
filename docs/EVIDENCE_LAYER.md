# Evidence Layer

## Purpose

The Evidence Layer ensures that every conclusion generated by CivilOS is supported by traceable evidence.

The goal is to reduce hallucinations, increase transparency, and improve trust in collective intelligence.

---

## Core Principle

No conclusion should exist without evidence.

Every important statement must be linked to supporting information.

---

## Evidence Structure

Each conclusion should include:

### Conclusion

The final recommendation or finding.

---

### Supporting Evidence

Facts, observations, research, and findings used to support the conclusion.

---

### Sources

The origin of supporting information.

Examples:

- Research reports
- Scientific literature
- Public datasets
- Government publications

---

### Confidence Score

A measure of confidence in the conclusion.

Example:

- High Confidence
- Medium Confidence
- Low Confidence

---

### Counterarguments

Challenges and objections generated by the Critic Agent.

---

## Agent Responsibilities

Research Agent:
Provides evidence.

Science Agent:
Validates evidence.

Economics Agent:
Evaluates impact evidence.

Policy Agent:
Reviews regulatory evidence.

Critic Agent:
Searches for contradictory evidence.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Create an evidence-first intelligence system where conclusions are transparent, explainable, and continuously verifiable.
