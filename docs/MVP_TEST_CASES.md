# MVP Test Cases

## Purpose

The purpose of these test cases is to verify that CivilOS produces validated knowledge through evidence, criticism, validation, memory, and collective reasoning.

These tests are designed to confirm that CivilOS behaves as a collective intelligence system rather than a traditional chatbot.

---

# Global Validation Rules

Every test case must verify:

- Evidence was collected.
- Evidence was validated.
- Criticism occurred.
- Weak hypotheses were rejected.
- Strong hypotheses survived review.
- Memory was used or updated.
- Knowledge Graph relationships were validated.
- Conclusions are traceable to evidence.
- No unsupported claims become permanent knowledge.

If any of these conditions fail:

TEST FAILED

---

# Test Case 1

## Objective

Verify that CivilOS can identify an emerging large-scale global challenge.

## User Question

What emerging global challenge could affect more than 100 million people by 2040 and why is it underestimated today?

## Expected Agent Activity

Research Agent:

- Collects evidence.
- Identifies candidate hypotheses.

Science Agent:

- Validates scientific credibility.

Economics Agent:

- Evaluates economic consequences.

Policy Agent:

- Evaluates governance implications.

Critic Agent:

- Challenges assumptions.

## Success Conditions

- At least one hypothesis is rejected.
- At least one hypothesis survives review.
- Evidence supports the final conclusion.
- Conclusion is traceable to sources.
- Memory Layer receives validated knowledge.
- Knowledge Graph records validated relationships.

## Failure Conditions

- No evidence collected.
- No criticism performed.
- Unsupported conclusion produced.
- Knowledge stored without validation.

# Test Case 2

## Objective

Verify that CivilOS can evaluate a causal relationship.

## User Question

Will water scarcity increase migration?

## Expected Agent Activity

Research Agent:

- Collects water scarcity evidence.
- Collects migration evidence.

Science Agent:

- Validates causal evidence.

Economics Agent:

- Evaluates economic effects.

Policy Agent:

- Evaluates governance consequences.

Critic Agent:

- Searches for weaknesses.

## Success Conditions

- Relationship is evidence-backed.
- Counterarguments are identified.
- Weak reasoning is challenged.
- Graph relationship is validated.
- Final conclusion remains traceable.

## Failure Conditions

- Relationship lacks evidence.
- Contradictory evidence ignored.
- Graph contains unsupported links.

# Test Case 3

## Objective

Verify that CivilOS can identify underestimated infrastructure risks.

## User Question

Which infrastructure risk is most underestimated today?

## Expected Agent Activity

Research Agent:

- Identifies candidate risks.

Science Agent:

- Validates technical credibility.

Economics Agent:

- Estimates economic consequences.

Policy Agent:

- Evaluates societal and regulatory impact.

Critic Agent:

- Challenges assumptions.

## Success Conditions

- Multiple risks are evaluated.
- Weak risks are rejected.
- Strong risks survive review.
- Memory receives validated discoveries.
- Final recommendation is evidence-backed.

## Failure Conditions

- Single unsupported answer produced.
- No criticism performed.
- No validation performed.
- No evidence supporting conclusions.

---

# System-Level Success Criteria

CivilOS is considered successful when:

- Every accepted conclusion has evidence.
- Every accepted conclusion survives criticism.
- Every accepted conclusion is validated by multiple agents.
- Every memory entry is traceable.
- Every graph relationship is evidence-backed.
- No fabricated metrics are displayed.
- No unsupported claims become permanent knowledge.
